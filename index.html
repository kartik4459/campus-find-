❓ QUICK HELP

Got a question? We've got you covered.

Find quick answers to common questions about reporting, verifying, and recovering items.

I lost something. What should I do?
+
Create a Lost Item report with the item's details, where and when you lost it. If a potential match is found, you can verify your ownership and connect with the finder.
I found someone's item. What should I do?
+
Create a Found Item report with the item's details and where you found it. If someone claims it, you can verify their ownership before connecting with them.
How is ownership verified?
+
The claimant provides a hidden detail about the item that isn't publicly displayed. The finder can use this detail to verify whether the claim is genuine.
When can I chat with the other user?
+
Chat becomes available only after the ownership claim has been approved.
How do I mark my item as recovered?
+
After the item has been returned, confirm that you received it. The item will then be marked as recovered.
Is my contact information protected?
+
You can coordinate through CampusFind's chat without immediately sharing personal contact information.
